FUNCTIONS

- sectors('GBR'): household vs corporate credit.
- mpp_table('KWT'): all tools: latest action, direction, previous/new level, change (pp), magnitude note.

INTERPRETATION
- Gap = ratio minus trend (pp of GDP); buffer guide 0% below 2 pp, rising to 2.5% at 10 pp. Positive, rising
  gap = credit outpacing trend (risk build-up). A guide, not a rule. Method, data length and references:
  README sheet of IMF_MFS_credit_to_GDP.xlsx; quote it with its citations.
- Tools: define in plain words from sheet Definitions (e.g. LTV, DSTI), with a country example.
